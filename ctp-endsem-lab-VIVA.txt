Coordination is not only about body movements; it also happens in our thinking and mental activities.

It helps us organize and control mental processes like attention, emotions, and intentions.

Cognitive coordination allows us to balance beliefs, ideas, and judgments in a clear and stable way.

It supports combining different thinking skills such as analytical (breaking down) and synthetic (connecting) thinking.

This improves problem-solving, decision-making, and overall understanding of situations.
